About this comparison

What a band difference means. The GEM-15 DTM is derived from its DSM by a machine-learning model, not measured separately, so the gap between the two panes is that model's output rather than an object height. DTM sits at or below DSM at every preset here. The size of the gap is not a proxy for canopy or building height on steep ground. The bands are mosaicked independently, and on a ridge that drops hundreds of metres across a few 9 m pixels a sub-pixel difference in where each band lands moves the number by tens of metres. Machu Picchu reads 92.25 m for that reason. Trust the flat points.

Experimental. The two GEM-15 panes read archives built on an unmerged fork, cholmes/mapterhorn tree gem-15-source, and discussed upstream in mapterhorn issue 368. They are not Mapterhorn releases.
