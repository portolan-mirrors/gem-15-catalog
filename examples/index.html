gem-15-catalog examples

Browser applications that read the GEM-15 Portolan catalog.

  • MapLibre terrain

    The terrarium-encoded elevation PMTiles of GEM-15 as a MapLibre raster-dem source. It draws a hypsometric tint, a hillshade, and 3D terrain, and it loads the published styles of the catalog.

  • Barcelona demo, live data

    The same MapLibre terrain example, pinned to the published Barcelona demo PMTiles archive (z0–14) instead of the global file.

The catalog is at source.coop/portolan-mirrors/gem-15-catalog. The source is at github.com/portolan-mirrors/gem-15-catalog.
