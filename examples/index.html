gem-15-catalog examples

Browser applications that read the GEM-15 Portolan catalog.

  • MapLibre terrain

    The terrarium-encoded elevation PMTiles of GEM-15 as a MapLibre raster-dem source. It draws a hypsometric tint, a hillshade, and 3D terrain, and it loads the published styles of the catalog.

  • Barcelona demo, live data

    The same MapLibre terrain example, pinned to the published Barcelona demo PMTiles archive (z0–14) instead of the global file.

  • Three terrain sources compared

    GEM-15 beside Mapterhorn and the AWS Terrain Tiles, in three synced panes over the same view. Click anywhere to read the elevation that each source gives, the zoom it came from, and the differences between them.

  • Mapterhorn today, and a GEM-15 build

    What Mapterhorn ships today beside GEM-15 run through their pipeline, over Barcelona, Cusco, and the High Atlas. Barcelona has their regional shard and Spanish lidar at z16. The other two areas have no shard, and the GEM-15 build reaches one zoom level deeper than production.

The catalog is at source.coop/portolan-mirrors/gem-15-catalog. The source is at github.com/portolan-mirrors/gem-15-catalog.
